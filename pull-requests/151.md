## What changed
- accept both Vinted category URL forms: `catalog[]` and canonical `/catalog/{id}-{slug}`
- stop probing the unrelated `filter-selection-button` after category selection
- keep exact `VINTED_MODEL` filtering fail-closed
- add regression coverage for query, encoded-query, canonical-path, empty, and lookalike-host URLs

## Why
The anonymous market observer can navigate to a canonical category path after selecting the leaf. The previous check only accepted `catalog[]`, so valid selections such as the tablet category were treated as failures and retried with a selector that is not present in that UI.
