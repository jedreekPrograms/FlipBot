## Description

## Problem
A one-step negotiation ladder is valid in the backend, and the negotiation decision logic can correctly finish/reject an active one-step conversation without another offer. `RealActionPreflight`, however, treated every negotiating product with fewer than two configured steps as a hard failure whenever real next steps were armed.

In a multi-product bot this meant one valid one-step conversation could downgrade real next-step submission to dry-run for every other product, including products that still had valid next steps to send.

## Changes
- keep one-step negotiation ladders valid during `NEGOTIATION_CHECK` preflight
- record a diagnostic note that such a conversation may finish without sending `NEXT_STEP`
- preserve all existing ladder validation for empty, malformed, non-sequential, or non-positive steps
- add regression coverage for:
  - a single valid one-step MAIN negotiation
  - an inactive one-step additional product coexisting with an active three-step product

## Safety
This does not force a next step where none exists. `NegotiationDecisionService` remains responsible for deciding whether a conversation has a valid next step; when the ladder is exhausted it closes/surfaces the conversation without submitting another offer.
