## Description

## What
- add one run-scoped catalog item-detail inspection budget with a hard limit of 25 live requests
- share that budget across MAIN and every active additional product in one `CATALOG_SCAN`
- keep the existing per-product 5 pre-check / 20 final-verification limits as secondary guards
- consume capacity only for uncached live item-page verification; exact current-model provenance and cached identity remain free
- defer budget-exhausted listings as `DISCOVERED` instead of assigning a false mismatch/unavailable/error status
- keep the legacy `NewNegotiationProcessor` constructor behavior equivalent by giving standalone processors their own 25-request budget
- add regression coverage for hard capacity and sharing across independent consumers

## Why
Before this change, each product received fresh 5+20 detail-page limits. MAIN plus four additional products could therefore multiply one catalog job's worst-case detail navigation work from 25 to roughly 125. The shared budget preserves single-product behavior while bounding multi-product Chromium/page churn per catalog run.
