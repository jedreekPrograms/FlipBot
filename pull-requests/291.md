## Description

## Why

A production log exposed two user-visible failures:

1. some S26 conversations started on Oct 4 sent one offer and then appeared silent;
2. bot 3 / S25 received a formal seller price (e.g. 1264 PLN) on Vinted but the conversation was no longer inspected, so it never reached "Oferty do kupienia".

The audit also found a third stall: an adaptive NEXT_STEP rejected by Vinted as below its live minimum was retried forever at the same invalid price.

## Root causes

- PENDING no-response timing multiplied the configured wait by 2, so a configured 24h step silently became 48h.
- scheduler only considered NEGOTIATING rows; a recently REJECTED/EXPIRED/technical-unavailable conversation could receive a later formal seller counteroffer and never be opened again.
- later adaptive steps had no live-minimum recovery even though first offers already supported adaptive Vinted-minimum handling.

## Fix

### Normal active negotiations
- non-final PENDING advances after the configured rejection wait, not 2x it;
- final-step 48h expiry is unchanged;
- formal seller counteroffers <= adaptive global cap still become ACTION_REQUIRED immediately.

### Bounded terminal conversation watch
- watches only recent technical terminal statuses: REJECTED, EXPIRED, UNAVAILABLE, CONTACT_UNAVAILABLE;
- 7-day window, recheck throttle 5 minutes, max 4 candidates per product/run;
- newest unseen terminal conversations are prioritized;
- active NEGOTIATING conversations are always processed first;
- recovery inspection is read-only and never sends an offer directly;
- a fresh affordable formal seller counteroffer/acceptance can become ACTION_REQUIRED immediately;
- non-final technical closures may reopen only when an immutable pinned strategy snapshot exists;
- legacy rows without a pinned strategy may surface a fresh seller price for manual purchase, but are never auto-reopened to send prices using today's strategy.

### Adaptive Vinted live minimum
- if a later adaptive step is rejected as too low and Vinted exposes the exact minimum, retry preparation at that live minimum;
- never exceed maxAutomaticOffer;
- quota/action guard is acquired only after the adjusted price prepares successfully;
- static mode remains fail-closed.

## Safety unchanged
- 25/day hard quota unchanged;
- real-action idempotency guard unchanged;
- no recovery path clicks Buy;
- no historical PURCHASED / user-skipped rows are watched;
- strategy versioning remains authoritative for started conversations.

## Regression coverage
- configured pending cadence including 24h-not-48h case;
- exact 1264 PLN parsing and 1264 <= 1450 ACTION_REQUIRED;
- terminal 1264 recovery;
- non-final pinned recovery vs legacy no-snapshot fail-closed;
- scheduler sees due recovery work even with zero NEGOTIATING rows;
- recovery preserves old step timers;
- live Vinted minimum 1080 adaptation and cap refusal;
- watched-status allowlist.
