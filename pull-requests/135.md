## Description

## What changed
- make `daily_offer_quota_reservation.usage_date` the source of truth for quota day attribution whenever a request-id reservation exists
- prevent an action reserved just before midnight and audited just after midnight from charging both quota days
- retain audit timestamp day attribution only for legacy audits that predate the reservation ledger
- add regression coverage for cross-midnight confirmation and same-day audit reconciliation

## Why
PR #134 made quota request-idempotent and audit-backed, but an audit created after midnight could otherwise be counted in the new day even when its persistent reservation belongs to the previous day. The hard `/25` safety limit must charge each real action exactly once to its reservation day.

## Safety semantics
- active reservation still counts conservatively
- CONFIRMED/AMBIGUOUS audits still form a durable floor
- released/legacy behavior is unchanged
- no quota slot can migrate to a different day just because audit persistence happens after midnight
