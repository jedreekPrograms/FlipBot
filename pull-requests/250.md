## Description

Full-audit correctness fix for fail-closed real-action auditing.

The NEXT_STEP audit coordinator re-resolved `offerPrice` from the bot's main configured step number. That can differ from the price actually prepared/submitted when adaptive pricing scales a step or when the negotiation belongs to an additional product with its own strategy.

The coordinator now receives the effective price directly from `decision.nextStep()` and records that value. This is especially important for AMBIGUOUS post-submit audits, where the audit record is the forensic source of truth. CONFIRMED backend auditing still independently derives its trusted final price from listing state.

No negotiation decision, submit, quota, action-guard or pricing policy behavior changes.
