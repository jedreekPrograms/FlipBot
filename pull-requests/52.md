## Description

## Why
The previous backlog fix removed future-step reservations completely. That makes the backend report raw remaining daily action quota as the number of new conversations, which is not the intended model.

The intended model is reservation-based:
- dailyNegotiationBudget is the daily pool of price actions
- a new conversation may start only when the pool can cover its entire configured negotiation ladder
- already-sent actions count against today's quota
- every active NEGOTIATING/ACTION_REQUIRED conversation reserves only its still-unsent steps
- if a conversation finishes early, its unused reservation is released immediately
- at midnight the actual-used counter resets for the new Warsaw day, but still-active conversations continue reserving their remaining future steps

Example with 25 daily actions and 5 configured steps:
- fresh day: 5 new full conversations fit
- five conversations that all finish after step 3 on the same day used 15 actions, so 10 actions remain and 2 more full conversations fit
- new day with three conversations still active at currentStep=3: they reserve 3*2=6 actions; 19 remain, so floor(19/5)=3 new conversations fit

## Changes
- restores reservation-aware NegotiationPlanner and removes the previous off-by-one behavior
- currentStep means the last already-sent step, so an active step-3 conversation in a 5-step ladder reserves exactly 2 future actions, not 3
- counts both NEGOTIATING and ACTION_REQUIRED as active reservations
- missing/invalid currentStep fails safe by reserving the full ladder
- NegotiationCapacityService combines today's actual remaining quota with active future-step reservations and divides the remainder by the full ladder size
- frontend budget copy now explains the reservation and midnight behavior
