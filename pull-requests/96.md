## Description

Stage 16 removes the now-unreachable backend negotiation lifecycle left behind after Stage 13 removed its legacy REST controller.

Evidence:
- the removed `NegotiationService` had no current caller after `/api/negotiations/*` was removed;
- `NegotiationEngine`, `NegotiationAction`, backend `NegotiationDecision`, `NegotiationResult`, `NegotiationResultRequest`, and `OfferSentRequest` formed only that obsolete internal island;
- backend negotiation tests cover the active capacity/planner/guard/quota architecture instead; no legacy-engine tests or clients remain.

Why remove it:
- the old lifecycle could mutate negotiation step/status without the current persistent guard/quota/browser-confirmation/audit guarantees;
- even though unreachable today, keeping an alternative unsafe lifecycle makes accidental future re-exposure easier and makes the commercial codebase harder to reason about.

Scope:
- seven backend legacy files deleted; deletions only;
- active `NegotiationPlanner`, `NegotiationCapacityService`, `NegotiationStep`, `NegotiationReactionAction`, seller counter-offer rules, current backend APIs, and all Playwright negotiation logic remain untouched.

Full CI is required to prove no hidden compile/runtime dependency remains.
