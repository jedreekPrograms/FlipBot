## Description

## What changed
- reconstruct the unstarted market queue at the latest complete scan, yesterday 24:00, and the start/end of the previous full week
- treat a listing as handled only after a confirmed `FIRST_OFFER` from a bot matching that model
- base the bot recommendation on realised weekly throughput plus queue growth/shrinkage instead of scaling against the whole observed opportunity set
- expose a signed bot balance: positive = missing bots, negative = surplus bots
- show queue history/trends and signed balance in Price Matrix
- add backend regression coverage for queue growth, shrinkage, and surplus sizing

## Why
The previous `started / all observed listings` ratio could suggest more bots even when existing bots still had free capacity. Queue trend distinguishes a real capacity shortage (queue grows) from spare capacity (queue shrinks / stays controlled).

The queue is reconstructed from observer presence (`firstSeenAt`/`lastSeenAt`) and confirmed FIRST_OFFER audit history for the model.
