## Description

## What
- clear per-bot catalog product rotation after a stopped bot schedule is fully removed
- clear per-bot negotiation rotation at the same lifecycle point
- clear consecutive contact-unavailable suspicion counters only for the stopped bot
- defer cleanup when STOP races with a claimed WORKING job until `completeRun()` finishes that claim
- deliberately keep process-wide real-offer one-shot safety sets untouched across STOP/START
- add regression coverage for catalog/negotiation rotation reset, per-bot contact-counter isolation, queued STOP cleanup, and WORKING STOP deferred cleanup

## Why
Small process-wide per-bot maps could retain entries for bots that had been stopped or deleted during a long-lived Playwright JVM. The state is tiny, but it should follow the scheduler lifecycle exactly. Cleanup must not happen while a claimed job is still executing, and it must never re-arm process-wide real-action safety gates.
