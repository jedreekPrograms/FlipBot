## Description

Full-audit lifecycle safety fix.

A stopped bot could be deletable while a FIRST_OFFER action was still unresolved but the listing remained DISCOVERED. Bot/listing deletion cascades the real_action_guard, while marketplace_negotiation_claim intentionally has no FK/cascade. That could orphan an unconfirmed global claim and block the marketplace listing indefinitely.

Deletion now fails closed when the bot owns either:
- any unresolved real_action_guard; or
- any unconfirmed marketplace_negotiation_claim.

Confirmed marketplace ownership remains durable after bot deletion, preserving cross-bot duplicate-negotiation protection. Added unit coverage for guard, unconfirmed claim, and normal safe deletion.
