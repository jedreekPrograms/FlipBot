## Description

Adds manual history corrections for cases where the final purchase price changed outside FlipBot after the bot's last observed negotiation step.

Changes:
- PURCHASED history cards can edit the final purchase price inline
- corrected price updates Listing.currentPrice, so dashboard spent/savings/averages recalculate automatically
- any history entry can be removed from the visible history
- removal is a soft hide (`history_hidden`) rather than deleting the listing row, preventing the bot from rediscovering and acting on the same marketplace listing again
- hidden history entries are also excluded from historical dashboard stats
- adds PATCH/DELETE history endpoints, Flyway V32, UI styling, and backend regression tests

Example: a phone recorded at 1100 PLN can be corrected to 750 PLN after a later manual seller concession.
