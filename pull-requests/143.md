## Description

## What changed
- keep the current adaptive scaling logic while the calculated next step stays below the hard cap
- when a later scaled step would exceed `maxAutomaticOffer`, clamp that step to the cap instead of ending the negotiation
- allow subsequent configured steps to continue at the same capped price, preserving their step number, message, timing and reaction flow
- keep the existing fail-closed behavior when the very first adaptive offer itself cannot fit under the hard cap
- keep the safety rule that lowering the cap below an already-sent offer never generates a lower follow-up

## Example
With a hard cap of 1350 PLN, a ladder that would scale to `1250 -> 1390 -> 1460` now becomes `1250 -> 1350 -> 1350` rather than stopping after step 1.

## Tests
- first adaptive offer still cannot cross the hard cap
- later scaled steps clamp to the cap
- later configured steps can remain at the same capped price
- decision flow advances to the next configured step at the capped price
- lowering the cap below an already-sent offer still stops automatic escalation
