## Full audit — stage 1: concurrency / real-action integrity

This stage fixes two correctness hazards found during the renewed production audit:

1. A temporary cross-bot FIRST_OFFER claim could mark a competing per-bot listing terminal and then fail to reopen it if the competitor held a row lock during `SKIP LOCKED` cleanup.
2. A stopped bot could be deleted while a real-action guard or unresolved marketplace claim still existed, destroying reconciliation evidence and leaving an orphaned global claim.

### Changes
- distinguish PRE-SUBMIT marketplace reservations from CONFIRMED durable claims (`V35`)
- temporary claim conflicts leave competing listings `DISCOVERED`
- only confirmed claims create `SKIPPED_ALREADY_NEGOTIATED`
- pre-submit release is now a single ownership-checked delete; no reopen/`SKIP LOCKED` race
- confirmed/stale FIRST_OFFER guard reconciliation marks the marketplace claim durable before guard cleanup
- bot deletion is blocked by active real-action guards or unconfirmed marketplace claims
- unit coverage for temporary vs confirmed claims and safe bot deletion
- PostgreSQL CI smoke coverage for V33/V34/V35 and the new claim lifecycle

Do not merge unless Backend, Playwright and Frontend CI are all green.
